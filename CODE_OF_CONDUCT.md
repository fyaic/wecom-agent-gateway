# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free project
community.

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and code on their merits;
- give actionable feedback and assume good intent;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and different levels of experience;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwanted disclosure
  of private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or a
GitHub private report. Good-faith reports will be handled discreetly and
without retaliation.
